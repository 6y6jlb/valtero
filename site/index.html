Valtero

Multi-currency personal expense tracker for Linux, Windows, and Android.

Track spending in the currencies you use, convert amounts when you need reporting totals, and keep your data on your device. Optional integrations let you sync an encrypted backup to Google Drive, export to Telegram, or refresh exchange rates from public providers.

Features

  • Expenses — amount, currency, payment method, country, category tags, notes, and date
  • Multi-currency — store amounts as-is or convert into a reporting currency with live rates
  • Dashboard & lists — charts and filters by country, payment, category, month, or currency
  • Google Drive Sync — optional end-to-end encrypted sync (user-enabled)
  • Export — CSV/JSON, encrypted backup files, optional Telegram destination
  • Rates — Frankfurter (ECB, no key) by default; optional ExchangeRate-API key
